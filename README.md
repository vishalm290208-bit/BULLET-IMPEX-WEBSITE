# Bullet Impex Private Limited - Official B2B Website

Clean, professional, and authentic B2B showcase website for **Bullet Impex Private Limited**, a premier cotton garment manufacturing & export house in Tirupur, India.

Designed with clean white space, refined slate typography, and zero artificial clutter—focused on real manufacturing capabilities, ethical SEDEX compliance, and direct buyer inquiries.

---

## 🏭 Company Profile & Core Data
- **Company**: Bullet Impex Private Limited
- **Location**: SF No. 343/2, 3D Nathar Thottam, Anna Nagar Extension, Kuthuspuram, Mannarai, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu 641607, India
- **Specialties**: Knitted Kids Wear (hoodies, pyjama sets, shorts, pant sets) and Adult Apparel (premium cotton tees, polo shirts, loungewear).
- **In-House Capabilities**: CAD pattern grading, placement screen printing, multi-head Tajima embroidery & appliqué, fabric testing lab, and stitching CMT lines with 100% calibrated needle detection.
- **Monthly Capacity**: 100,000+ Finished Pieces / Month
- **Sampling Speed**: 7 to 10 Working Days
- **Compliance**: SEDEX (SMETA) 4-Pillar Audited (Ref ID: `ZC421899120`)
